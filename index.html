About Me

Design & Security Creed

“Security does not have to be punitive, and interface elegance shouldn't come at the cost of vulnerability. The best systems are impenetrable by attackers and effortless for humans.”

Hi, I'm Feath — exploring the twin frontiers of defensive cyber security and cutting-edge digital product aesthetics.

With a deep-seated fascination for vulnerability analysis, network telemetry, and cryptography on one side, and high-fidelity interaction design, tokenized systems, and intuitive wireframing on the other, I craft secure-by-default software that users genuinely love to experience.

Whether conducting threat modeling sessions, hardening microservices against injection attacks, or orchestrating responsive design systems in Figma and code, I build with precision, care, and continuous curiosity.
